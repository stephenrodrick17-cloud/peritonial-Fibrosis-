We examined GSE248762, a 10x single-cell RNA-sequencing dataset of overnight peritoneal dialysis effluent, containing 16 samples (SV n = 6, LV_NOT_UF n = 6, LV_UF n = 4; the pseudobulk contrast combined the long-vintage groups as LV (n = 10) versus SV (n = 6). After applying the prespecified cell filter, 110,353 of 118,895 cells were retained. The project shared-code consensus hub panel comprised 9 genes (TNFSF15, FLT3LG, EBI3, LTB, ADAM19, NAT6, ECM1, CST7, CCBE1); 5 were members of the five-gene model panel and 5 were members of the seven-gene consensus panel. 8 of the 9 hub genes (ADAM19, CCBE1, CST7, EBI3, ECM1, FLT3LG, LTB, TNFSF15) were testable in at least one lineage under the prespecified detection filter; NAT6 was not testable because it was absent from the expression feature table. Across the 26 testable gene-lineage-contrast combinations, 9 had nominal P < 0.05 and 4 passed global FDR < 0.05 (minimum FDR = 0.00952). Nominal-only P < 0.05 directional trends (not FDR-significant) were: EBI3 was directionally higher in LV than SV in lymphoid (log2FC = 1.455, P = 0.0225, FDR = 0.073); TNFSF15 was directionally higher in LV than SV in myeloid (log2FC = 1.963, P = 0.0123, FDR = 0.0642); EBI3 was directionally higher in LV than SV in myeloid (log2FC = 1.411, P = 0.0186, FDR = 0.0691); CCBE1 was directionally lower in LV than SV in fibroblast/stromal (log2FC = -2.102, P = 0.0362, FDR = 0.105); CCBE1 was directionally lower in LV than SV in mesothelial (log2FC = -1.621, P = 0.0155, FDR = 0.0673). Contrasts passing global FDR < 0.05 were ECM1 in myeloid (log2FC = 6.124, P = 0.00423, FDR = 0.0275); ECM1 in mesothelial (log2FC = 8.308, P = 0.000951, FDR = 0.00952); ECM1 in epithelial (log2FC = 6.603, P = 0.000986, FDR = 0.00952); CCBE1 in epithelial (log2FC = -2.259, P = 0.0011, FDR = 0.00952). These pseudobulk comparisons use samples as the replicate unit and provide an exploratory cellular contextualization, not validation of the diagnostic models; the small number of samples per group limits power.
