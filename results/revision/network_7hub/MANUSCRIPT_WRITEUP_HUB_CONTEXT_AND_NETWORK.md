# Single-cell transcriptomic contextualization of hub genes and candidate RNA network

## Single-cell transcriptomic contextualization of hub genes

### Methods

GSE248762 is a single-cell RNA-sequencing dataset of peritoneal dialysis effluent. The GEO design uses UF to mean ultrafiltration failure and defines LV_UF as peritoneal dialysis (PD) for more than 90 months with modified peritoneal equilibration test (Mod-PET) ultrafiltration volume below 400 mL; LV_NOT_UF is PD for more than 90 months with Mod-PET ultrafiltration volume above 400 mL, while SV is PD for less than 6 months (GEO accession GSE248762, Series overall design; PMID 39687014). The analysis used the seven validated hubs and sample-level pseudobulk counts. Long-vintage (LV) samples, comprising LV_NOT_UF and LV_UF, were pooled and compared with short-vintage (SV) samples; the contrast was LV minus SV, so positive log2 fold changes indicate higher abundance in LV. The saved QC audit included 10 LV samples (6 LV_NOT_UF and 4 LV_UF) and 6 SV samples. Samples with fewer than 20 cells in a lineage were excluded. The fitted model used edgeR quasi-likelihood methods, `filterByExpr`, robust dispersion estimation, and robust `glmQLFit`; Benjamini-Hochberg (BH) correction was applied to the 23 testable hub-gene/lineage comparisons. Of 49 possible pairs, 23 were testable, 6 had nominal P < 0.05, and 3 had BH q < 0.05. (`../single_cell_context/sample_group_qc_audit.csv`; `sc7_hub_gene_lineage_results.csv`; `../single_cell_context/04_pseudobulk_edger.R`)

The fold-change audit used the saved sample-level results. The edgeR and earlier Monte Carlo outputs were produced by an earlier full run; this report was regenerated in metadata-only mode from those saved outputs. No edgeR fit or permutation was rerun for this manuscript update. The earlier Monte Carlo outputs are superseded by exact enumeration of all valid label allocations; exact two-sided P values were 0.171453546453546 (myeloid), 0.000999000999000999 (mesothelial), and 0.0024975024975025 (epithelial). The report's verdict categories were revised so a result is not called robust if it failed BH correction or has no permutation test. (`../sc7_fc_audit/sc7_pair_verdicts.csv`; `../sc7_fc_audit/sc7_exact_permutation_summary_myeloid.csv`; `../sc7_fc_audit/sc7_exact_permutation_summary_mesothelial.csv`; `../sc7_fc_audit/sc7_exact_permutation_summary_epithelial.csv`; `../sc7_fc_audit/run_metadata.json`; `../sc7_fc_audit/FC_AUDIT_REPORT.md`)

### Interpretation

The results support an exploratory association between LV group and higher expression of one hub gene across three lineages, not three independent confirmations. The per-lineage edgeR effects have BH q < 0.05, but the largest single LV CPM sample supplied more than half of group CPM in each lineage, and the myeloid exact permutation test did not support the edgeR P value. Full exact enumeration yielded P = 0.000999000999000999 for mesothelial and P = 0.0024975024975025 for epithelial cells; the earlier Monte Carlo estimates are superseded and are not used for inference. The apparent pooled LV contrast may also reflect the LV_UF/LV_NOT_UF mixture: subgroup mean CPM was much higher in LV_UF, with a small eligible n and influential individual samples. Remaining nominal pairs did not survive BH correction; two have no permutation test. Sample sizes are small, the same GSM samples recur across lineages, and cell-level observations are not independent biological replicates. These results are exploratory and do not constitute clinical validation. (`sc7_hub_gene_lineage_results.csv`; `../sc7_fc_audit/sc7_pair_verdicts.csv`; `../sc7_fc_audit/sc7_exact_permutation_summary_myeloid.csv`; `../sc7_fc_audit/sc7_exact_permutation_summary_mesothelial.csv`; `../sc7_fc_audit/sc7_exact_permutation_summary_epithelial.csv`; `../sc7_fc_audit/sc7_ecm1_subgroup_summary.csv`; `../single_cell_context/sample_group_qc_audit.csv`)

## lncRNA-miRNA-mRNA regulatory network

### Methods

The network is a database-derived candidate model. A high-confidence axis required Tier A or Tier B miRNA-mRNA evidence and ENCORI CLIP support for the lncRNA-miRNA edge with at least two supporting experiments. Other assembled axes are lower-confidence background. This rule defines evidence tiers; it does not demonstrate regulation in peritoneal dialysis samples. (`network_reconciliation.csv`; `hub_miRNA_tier_counts.csv`)

For GSE248762, the lncRNA-hub co-expression analysis used sample-level pseudobulk log2(CPM + 1) and Spearman correlations within lineage. The adjusted analysis was a partial Spearman correlation controlling for the rank of log(1 + lineage cell count), intended to assess whether an observed lncRNA-hub association remained after accounting for the number of cells contributing to each lineage pseudobulk. BH correction was applied over the testable pairs for the adjusted and unadjusted analyses separately. (`lncRNA_detection.csv`; `lncRNA_mRNA_correlation_summary.csv`; `build_network_7hub.py`)

### Results

The candidate set contains 323,461 lncRNA-miRNA-mRNA axes across 7 hubs, 172 miRNAs, and 14,950 lncRNAs. Of these, 1,260 met the high-confidence definition and 322,201 were lower-confidence background. These are candidate axes, not validated regulatory relationships. (`network_reconciliation.csv`)

Of 14,950 candidate lncRNAs, 1,271 passed the GSE248762 detection threshold (at least 1.0% of QC-retained cells and at least 3 samples); this is 8.501672240802675% of the candidate set. Only that subset entered the defined pseudobulk co-expression analysis. The adjusted analysis tested 38,446 pairs using cell-count-adjusted partial Spearman correlation. Fourteen pairs had global BH q < 0.05 after this adjustment. These results test adjusted co-expression and do not establish directionality, physical interaction, miRNA mediation, or regulation of any hub transcript. (`lncRNA_detection.csv`; `lncRNA_mRNA_correlation_summary.csv`)

The multiMiR predicted query returned exactly 1,000 rows and may be truncated; completeness is NOT VERIFIED. ENCORI ceRNA queries were not retrieved for 4 of 7 hubs. Six GSE182736/PRJNA757643 sample run records were present in saved ENA metadata, but the raw reads were not quantified into sample-level miRNA abundance. The local miRNA workbook was verified to contain annotation/sequence fields (`Item` and `Description`), not sample-level expression. Therefore, sample-level miRNA expression in peritoneal samples is NOT VERIFIED, and miRNA detection was not used to filter the network axes. (`database_source_versions.csv`; `ENCORI_ceRNA_query_status.csv`; `miRNA_expression_support.csv`; `GSE182736_workbook_audit.csv`)

The single-cell co-expression analysis does not validate the proposed lncRNA-miRNA-mRNA network. It can nominate lncRNA-hub co-expression candidates after adjustment for cell number, but it does not show that miRNAs are expressed in these samples or that any lncRNA regulates a hub through a shared miRNA. The network should be described as a hypothesis-generating candidate list.

### Limitations

The pseudobulk sample sizes are small. The same GSM samples recur across lineages, but participant identifiers are not reported, so samples may not be independent donors and donor-level independence cannot be verified. Cell-level observations are not independent biological replicates. Pooling LV_UF and LV_NOT_UF may obscure subgroup differences and may contribute to the pooled LV-versus-SV association. The exact-enumeration results sharpen the interpretation: myeloid exact P = 0.171453546453546, while mesothelial and epithelial exact P values are 0.000999000999000999 and 0.0024975024975025. BH q-values for the DecontX contrasts are NOT VERIFIED and are not reported. LncRNA detection is sparse: 1,271 of 14,950 candidates passed the stated threshold. The regulatory network relies on database evidence with incomplete query coverage, a potentially truncated 1,000-row predicted result, four unretrieved ENCORI ceRNA hub queries, and no verified sample-level miRNA expression. Accordingly, neither the single-cell results nor the database network constitute clinical validation or proof of post-transcriptional regulation. The GEO series matrix reports Cell Ranger v6.1.1 processing for all samples but contains no batch field, so processing-batch effects could not be assessed.
